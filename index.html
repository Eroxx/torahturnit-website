This week's Torah portion, a few minutes a day.

“Turn it and turn it, for everything is in it.” A rabbi named Ben Bag Bag said that about the Torah nearly two thousand years ago, and it’s where this app gets its name.

Every week brings a new portion to turn over. Torah: Turn It gives you the story, a question worth arguing about, a surprise hidden in the Hebrew, and the text itself, a few minutes a day.

No Hebrew needed. Nothing watered down.
