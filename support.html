Support

Torah: Turn It is made by one person, a rabbi who wanted the weekly portion to be something you can keep up with a few minutes a day. Questions, corrections and ideas are all welcome.

Email

Which portion is it showing?

This week's, counting from Shabbat. Some weeks Israel and the diaspora read different portions; set "Where you live" in Settings and the app follows that calendar.

Do I need to know Hebrew?

No. Everything reads in English. If you want the Hebrew, the reader can show it with vowels, or with the trope marks for chanting.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Your place is kept, and a missed reading just waits for you. The week moves on with the calendar, and you can open any portion from the year view.

How do I find my bar or bat mitzvah portion?

Enter a birthday in the app and it works out the portion, then shows where it falls in the year.

My highlights aren't on my other device.

Highlights sync through iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, then open the app on each. It can take a minute.

I found a mistake in the text or a guide.

Please write, with the portion and verse. The English is the 1917 Jewish Publication Society translation, and every guide is checked against it.
